FOR SREE LEKHA

Dear Sree Lekha,

I don't really know where to start, because some people become so important that normal words never feel like enough. You are one of those people for me.

When I look back at everything we've shared, I remember the laughs, conversations, surprises, little acts of care, and all those ordinary moments that somehow became special because you were part of them.

From meeting in the library to becoming close, working together at OLA, celebrating birthdays, going on random plans, and making countless memories, our friendship has become a beautiful part of my life.

Even though life is taking us through different paths now, I hope nothing changes the memories we've created and the friendship we've built.

I genuinely hope this new year brings you happiness, success, beautiful opportunities, and everything you are working towards.

Today is your birthday, but honestly, I feel lucky that our paths crossed and that a simple conversation in a library became a friendship like this.

Thank you for every laugh, every memory, every surprise, every meal, every random moment, and every little way you cared.

Happy Birthday, Sree Lekha. ❤️

With lots of love, Tej Bhargav ♡
